# Public-Data Recruitment Decision Support Portfolio

## Problem

Football recruitment decisions are made under uncertainty: budget limits, tactical needs, player availability, league translation risk and incomplete information. Public data cannot replace club intelligence, but it can structure the first screening layer and make assumptions transparent.

## Approach

This MVP builds a role-based recruitment workflow:

1. Public-data squad need hypothesis
2. Role-based longlist generation
3. Customisable shortlist scoring
4. Value-for-money and reliability checks
5. Player-level QA flags for scout/video/medical validation

## Demonstration case

The included demo uses a Celtic-style January recruitment scenario with priority roles at CB, right-sided FB/WB and ST. The scenario is illustrative and should not be read as an internal club recommendation.

## Value for clubs

- Makes early recruitment screening faster and more transparent.
- Separates hard evidence from assumptions.
- Allows tactical and financial weights to be changed by club context.
- Produces player cards that are easy to discuss with scouts, coaches and recruitment leadership.
- Creates a clear handoff from data screening to video and live scouting.

## Limitations

This is a public-data decision-support demo. It does not include internal tactical plans, medical records, exact wages, agent context, transfer availability, personality assessment or negotiation constraints.

## Portfolio outputs

- Methodology report
- Reproducible notebook
- Configurable scoring pipeline
- Sample longlist and shortlist
- Player cards and QA flags
