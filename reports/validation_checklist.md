# Recruitment QA and Video Validation Checklist

## Purpose

The shortlist is not a final recruitment decision. It is a prioritisation layer. Each candidate should be validated through video, scout judgement, medical checks, contract/wage checks and tactical discussion.

## QA categories

| Category | Question | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Role fit | Does the player match the role profile, not just the position label? | Green / Amber / Red |
| Reliability | Are the minutes sufficient to trust per-90 outputs? | Green / Amber / Red |
| Availability | Does public injury/absence history raise concern? | Green / Amber / Red |
| Value | Is the public value proxy consistent with the modelled performance/value signal? | Green / Amber / Red |
| League translation | Is the move a major step-up in league context? | Green / Amber / Red |
| Data coverage | Are key metrics missing for this role? | Green / Amber / Red |
| Video confirmation | Do clips support the data claim? | Green / Amber / Red |

## Role-specific video checks

### RB/RWB

- Does the player provide width or move inside?
- Are overlaps/underlaps timed well?
- Does the player progress the ball under pressure?
- What is the recovery-run quality after attacks?
- Are crosses/cutbacks selected intelligently?

### CB

- Can the player defend large spaces?
- Does the player progress the ball through passing or carrying?
- Is the player comfortable receiving under pressure?
- How reliable is body orientation when defending crosses?
- Is aerial dominance context-dependent or robust?

### ST

- Does shot quality come from repeatable movement?
- Does the player attack the box early enough?
- Is pressing active or tactically disciplined?
- Can the player link play or only finish?
- Are goals inflated by penalties, weak opposition or unsustainable finishing?

## Final recommendation labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Proceed | Data and video support further recruitment work |
| Proceed with conditions | Candidate is interesting but needs a specific check |
| Hold | Insufficient evidence or high uncertainty |
| Reject | Role mismatch or risk outweighs signal |
