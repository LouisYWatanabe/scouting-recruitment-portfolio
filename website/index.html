What this project shows

The aim is not to claim that public data can make final recruitment decisions. The aim is to show how public data can structure the first screening layer and make assumptions transparent before specialist human validation.

01

Squad need hypothesis

Estimate where a squad may need reinforcement using depth, minutes, age profile and role benchmarks.

02

Role-based longlist

Filter players by broad role family, age, minutes, value proxy and availability-related signals.

03

Configurable shortlist

Rank candidates with transparent weights for role performance, value, reliability and club context.

04

QA and validation flags

Highlight low-minutes, value, injury-history and data-coverage risks before final judgement.

Methodology and boundaries

This is deliberately framed as decision support, not automated recruitment advice. The strongest version combines transparent scoring, reproducible public data, sensitivity checks and human-in-the-loop validation.

DataBundled demo CSV, optional StatsBomb Open Data mode, and uploadable player-level CSVs for future club-specific datasets.
Role comparisonPlayers are compared within broad role families rather than across all positions. Fine-grained tactical labels require event or tracking data.
ScoringThe app combines role performance, squad complementarity, value intelligence, reliability and league-risk penalty using editable weights.
ValidationOutputs are treated as scouting hypotheses and QA prompts. Video, medical, wage, contract and tactical validation remain outside the public-data MVP.
